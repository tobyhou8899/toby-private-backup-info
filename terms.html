Use information

Toby Private Backup is a personal, owner-operated desktop backup connection. It has no public registration, paid subscription, or service offering to third parties.

Successful backup of a selected batch does not imply that every file or the entire computer has been backed up. Backup completeness and recoverability depend on the recorded scope, current credentials, and successful recovery tests. The owner retains separate access to the recovery password.
